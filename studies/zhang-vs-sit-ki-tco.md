# Passed on Zhang Minhua: two-season TCO vs. keeping Sit Ki Poon

Added 2026-09-25. Static decision record, not recomputed by the daily job.

**Decision: don't buy.** Zhang Minhua costs about double what keeping Sit Ki Poon costs over two seasons. He's the better player, but not by enough to justify that.

## Method

- **Horizon:** 26 Monday paydays from 2026-09-25: the 9 left this season, all 13 of next season and the first 4 of the season after (see [Season structure](season-structure.md)).
- **Zhang's salary** declines each season on the decay curve from [Bench-player acquisitions](bench-player-tco.md): `% change ≈ 14.1% − 4.02% × ln(salary)`, about −27% per season at his salary.
- **Sit Ki's salary stays flat:** he isn't being trained, so it won't rise.
- **Sunk costs are left out.** The $44,000 purchase and salary already paid on Sit Ki are gone either way.

**Calculated.** Zhang costs about **$275k more** over two seasons. If his salary is the $23,048 figure, his TCO is about $536k, still about $232k more.

## Why the gap is really larger

- **The bid could still rise.** $57,200 was the bid when assessed, and each extra dollar adds to his total.
- **Resale.** Sit Ki at 30 should still resell near his purchase price. Zhang at 42 would be worth very little.
- **Different roles.** Zhang is a veteran inside scorer and Sit Ki is a PG, so Zhang isn't a direct substitute.

## When the answer would change

Buying a player like Zhang only makes sense as a short-term push to win now: bought cheap and sold before his skills and value fall much. It doesn't make sense as a two-season hold at close to double the cost.

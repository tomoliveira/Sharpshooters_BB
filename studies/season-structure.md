# Season structure: Monday and Friday resets

Added 2026-09-10. Moved here from the report's Investments tab on 2026-09-24.

**Official · game schedule mechanics.** A season runs **14 weeks**, but its two weekly resets don't both land 14 times:

- **13 Monday resets.** TV money, merchandise and player salaries are all paid on this reset. This is what "one season of salary" means in every cost analysis in this knowledge base.
- **14 Friday resets.** Training progress and the weekly minutes tracker reset here instead.

Any dollar figure scaled per season uses **13**, not 14. A season's salary bill is one payday short of what a flat 14-week count would suggest.

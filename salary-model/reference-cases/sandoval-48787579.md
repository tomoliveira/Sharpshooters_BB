## Why this is useful

Seasons 59–68 show a decade-long plateau (stable skills, prime years — salary recalculates once per season purely from skills, so a flat salary means flat skills). Seasons 69–73 show a steep decline (73% salary loss in 5 seasons) as skills decayed with age.

The deceleration in the last transition (−30.8% → −15.0%) lines up with his current skills mostly sitting in the low/flat regime of the buzzer-manager.com per-skill curves (see `skill-curves-reference.md` in this same folder) — most further skill decay from here should move salary less, not more, since the steep part of each skill's curve is behind him. This is a useful sanity check on any future age/decay modeling: don't assume a constant % decline rate late in a player's career.
